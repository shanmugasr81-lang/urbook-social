# Background photos for urbook social posts

Since 15 Oct 2026 every post (Wednesday and Saturday) shows a natural photo behind the text instead of the navy brand colour.
`index.json` lists every usable photo with its category (`parking`, `airport`, `flight`, `transfer`, `holiday`), a short subject and its licence.

- **Pexels photos** (`backgrounds/<category>/pexels-<id>.jpg`): Pexels License – free for commercial use, no attribution required. The source page for each is in `index.json`. Chosen with no recognisable faces.
- **Shan's own photos** are referenced in place (`holiday/photos/…`, `transfers/photos/…`) – never moved or renamed.
- Rules: never a photo with a recognisable face, a readable number plate, or another company's logo as the subject. Add new photos to the owner folders or here, then add a line to `index.json`.
