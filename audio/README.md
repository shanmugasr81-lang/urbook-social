# urbook sound beds (royalty-free, original)

Synthesised from scratch by `tools/urbook_sound.py` (29 Sep 2026) – no samples, no third-party music, so
safe on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube (no licence, no Content ID claims).

| File | Mood | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| soft-travel.m4a | warm pad + gentle plucks | parking / transfers, everyday posts |
| airport-calm.m4a | soft pad, airport PA chime at the start, distant jet swells | Meet & Greet, Park & Ride, Self Park, airport news |
| sunrise-journey.m4a | brighter pad + soft pulse | holidays – Nepal, Sri Lanka, South India, itineraries |
| lounge-evening.m4a | mellow low pad + slow bells | Park, Sleep & Fly, early flights, polls |

Add to a video: `python3 tools/urbook_sound.py mux in.mp4 audio/<bed>.m4a out.mp4`
