## Rules
- Every reel: urbook logo, one offer (URFIRST10 first booking / URBACK5 returning), end card 'Book at urbooktravel.com'.
- Any voice is fine (Shan, 25 Nov 2026); tick the platform's AI-generated label when the voice is synthetic (Meta 'AI info', TikTok 'AI-generated content').
- No people on video without a signed consent form. No AI-made people. Footage is urbook-owned or free-licence only.
- Facts only from the package (itinerary, inclusions). No prices unless they match the live site. Flights not included.
- Nothing is posted without Shan's approval (Metricool review queue).
